The Guild Legacy

The Craft of
Nool & Zari

In classical Tamil, nool means the sacred thread of cotton or raw silk. Zari represents the ancient Persian art of flattening pure silver and molten gold wire to weave with the celestial.

For over four decades, our private atelier has preserved these 600-year-old pit loom legacies across South India and the holy banks of Varanasi, resisting synthetic shortcuts in favor of generational patience.
