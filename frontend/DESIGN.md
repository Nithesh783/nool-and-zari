## Brand & Style

This design system channels regal dusk, heirloom craftsmanship, and high-fashion romance. It balances the timeless poise of Indian bridal couture with modern architectural editorial design. The aesthetic combines clean, gallery-grade minimalism with tactile jewel-box opulence—pairing generous negative space and disciplined typography with rich, deep-toned canvases, shimmering metallic accents, and tender rose quartz undertones.

The interface serves discerning global patrons, bridal couples, collectors, and bespoke couture clients. It aims to evoke grandeur, quiet exclusivity, architectural grace, and reverent craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from twilight bridal processions, hand-loomed brocades, and vintage jewels:

- **Primary (`#111E38` / `#1A2E56`):** Midnight Indigo & Royal Sapphire. Evokes deep dusk sky, silk velvet, and rich indigo dye. Used for primary interactions, high-contrast text, structured lines, and noble surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#D8827A`):** Rosewater Silk & Blush Rose Quartz. Adds warmth, feminine grace, and subtle romance to bridal badges, seasonal accents, hover transitions, and micro-delights.
- **Tertiary (`#C0B298` / `#D4AF37`):** Platinum Zari & Pale Champagne Gold. Represents metallic brocade threads and heirloom jewelry. Utilized for borders, filigree dividers, monograms, and luxury status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#FDF7F5` / `#F5ECE8`):** Antique Basra Pearl & Warm Silk. The luminous base canvas that provides editorial warmth without sterile starkness.

Text uses Deep Obsidian (`#0A111F`) and Deep Midnight Indigo (`#111E38`) to maintain authoritative contrast against pearl and rosewater silk backdrops.

## Typography

The type system creates tension between theatrical classicism and razor-sharp modern precision:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers the vertical drama, fine serifs, and high-contrast chisel of fashion publications. Used for editorial storytelling, garment names, collection themes, and quotes. Italicized cuts may be applied to single evocative adjectives or French/Sanskrit sartorial terms.
- **Body & Captions (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry and balanced legibility. Set with comfortable line height and relaxed tracking to offset Bodoni’s expressive contrast.
- **Labels & Overlines (`Manrope` Caps):** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter tracking (`0.14em`–`0.18em`). Used for couture category tags, curation markers, pricing, craftsmanship specs, and navigation anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid with expansive negative space reminiscent of an art monograph:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Margins of `5rem` (`margin-desktop`) and gutters of `2.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`). Asymmetric column spans (e.g., 5-column imagery against 7-column prose) emphasize bespoke curation. Max container width is fixed at `1440px` centered.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Margins of `2.5rem` (`margin-tablet`) and gutters of `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Margins of `1.25rem` (`margin`) and gutters of `1rem`. Full-bleed editorial photography transitions gracefully with inset card layouts.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Macro spaces use `space-xl` and `space-2xl` between editorial acts, allowing couture garment photography breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial dropshadows in favor of tonal layering, metallic zari thread borders, and translucent veil surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Basra Pearl (`#FDF7F5`) for daylight modes; Velvet Midnight Indigo (`#111E38`) for evening runway and VIP salons.
- **Level 1 (Atelier Cards & Loupes):** Surface color of Antique Silk (`#F5ECE8`) bound by a hairline 1px border of Gilded Platinum Zari (`#C0B298`, 35% opacity).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Modals):** Frosted rosewater silk glassmorphism (`rgba(253, 247, 245, 0.92)` with `16px` backdrop blur) flanked by an ambient shadow tinted in indigo: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(17, 30, 56, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Bridal Trunk & Quick Inquiries):** Deep Midnight Indigo surfaces flanked by subtle Champagne Gold border trims (`#D4AF37`, 40% opacity) and an ambient diffusion: `0 32px 64px -16px rgba(17, 30, 56, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a sharp, architectural silhouette (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Rectilinear shapes evoke framed artwork, traditional wooden loom beams, and uncut gemstones.
- Buttons, input fields, image viewports, drawer panels, and modal containers retain strict right-angled geometry (`0px` border radius).
- Softness is introduced through typography, fluid fabric movement, delicate 1px hairline separators, and circular jewel buttons (e.g., swatch selectors, pagination pips, and audio toggles).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Atelier Button:** Solid Midnight Indigo (`#111E38`) background with Basra Pearl text (`#FDF7F5`) and uppercase tracking (`label-lg`). Pure rectangular edge with 1px border of `#111E38`. Hover transitions softly to Royal Sapphire (`#1A2E56`) with a 1px inner hairline of Platinum Zari (`#C0B298`).
- **Secondary / Bridal Consultation Button:** Transparent base framed in 1px Platinum Zari (`#C0B298`), text in Midnight Indigo. On hover, background shifts to faint Rosewater Silk (`rgba(216, 130, 122, 0.08)`) and border shifts to Pale Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Text Link:** Bodoni italic or Manrope uppercase with a permanent 1px hairline underline offset by `6px`, transitioning from Rose Quartz to Indigo upon hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict square-cut tags with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Pale Rose Quartz fill (`rgba(216, 130, 122, 0.15)`) with Midnight Indigo text for limited edition artisanal tags ("Heirloom Zari", "Handwoven Kanjeevaram", "Bespoke Only").

### Cards
- **Product & Lookbook Cards:** Clean rectangular frames with edge-to-edge imagery. Neutral background (`#F5ECE8`) with a hairline border (`#C0B298`, 30% opacity). Typography is bottom-aligned with garment title in `headline-sm` Bodoni and craft lineage in `label-sm` Manrope uppercase.
- Image zoom interactions perform an effortless scale (`scale(1.03)`) inside the strict rectangular clipping container.

### Input Fields
- Underline-only or flat framed architectural fields with 1px border in Platinum Zari (`#C0B298`).
- Label positioned in uppercase (`label-sm`), floating above on focus.
- Active focus state sharpens the bottom border to Midnight Indigo (`#111E38`) accompanied by a subtle Rosewater glow (`rgba(216, 130, 122, 0.25)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square selectors for checkboxes; diamond or square orientation for luxury filter options.
- Unselected: 1px border of Platinum Zari (`#C0B298`).
- Selected: Midnight Indigo fill containing an inset Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) check or dot.

### Specialized Couture Components
- **Zari Swatch & Karigari Magnifier:** Circular swatch toggle showing high-resolution metallic yarn weaves; clicking triggers a side-docked loupe with thread count details.
- **Bespoke Appointment Timeline:** A continuous vertical hairline in Platinum Zari with diamond nodes signifying measurement, fitting, embroidery proofing, and hand-delivery.
